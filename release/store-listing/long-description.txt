AliasNest is a private inbox for the disposable email addresses you give out to the world.

Sign up for a service. Order a package. Test a newsletter. You don't have to hand over your real email anymore — create a mask in AliasNest, hand it out, and read the mail in a calm, ad-free inbox that's only ever yours.


HOW IT WORKS

— Create unlimited aliases on the shared aliasnest.com domain, or bring your own (custom domains are first-class, verified via TXT and MX records).
— Mail sent to the alias arrives in your AliasNest inbox in seconds. Push notifications optional.
— Reply or forward — the recipient sees the alias, not your real address. Your inbox stays private.
— Pause or delete an alias the moment it starts attracting noise.


WHY PEOPLE USE IT

— Stop a sender by deleting the alias they wrote to. No unsubscribe forms, no spam folders.
— Compartmentalize: a fresh alias per service means you can tell which leak came from where.
— Keep your real inbox quiet and your real address out of marketing databases.
— Use plus-addressing on any alias (e.g., shopping1+amazon@aliasnest.com).


WHAT'S IN THE APP

— Unified inbox across every alias you've created, with per-alias filtering.
— Full HTML email rendering with images, links, and original formatting preserved.
— Reply, reply-all, and forward — formatting preserved, original message quoted.
— Attachment support: open inbound, send outbound.
— Per-alias display names so your replies look right ("Vijay Agarwal" instead of a raw address).
— Compose new mail directly from any alias.
— Custom domain support with TXT and MX verification built in.
— Light, editorial design. No ads, no tracking, no AI features bolted on.


PRIVACY

We don't sell, share, or feed your mail into a training set. Messages live on a server we run. Mail in transit travels over TLS but is stored unencrypted on the server like any standard mail service — treat it the way you'd treat any other private inbox.
